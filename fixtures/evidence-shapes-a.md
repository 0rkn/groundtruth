# Evidence line exemplars — Brambleside

First pass across all four themes, drafted against client set A. The five shapes are the
client's; these extend them. Every line is a **juxtaposition** — two facts in tension —
because a single restated fact ("the Board has nine seats") carries no finding.

Sources use `document:page`. `PR` = `05-previous-board-review.pdf`, which may supply a
**commitment or a date** but never a finding, and may never be the only term in a line.

Confidence: **firm** = both terms verbatim or arithmetic, tension is in the documents.
**check** = flagged for correction, reason given.

## Resources

**res-01** · Board and committee packs are concise, timely and decision ready. · shape b · firm
> Your July 2026 pack ran to 247 pages across 14 papers and was issued four working days
> before the meeting, against your own standard of seven.

`03:1` (pack size, issue date, stated standard), `04:4` (seven calendar days)

**res-02** · Information is received with enough lead time for proper consideration. · shape d · firm
> Your 2023 review recommended a 120 page pack limit. Your July 2026 pack is 247 pages,
> and papers were issued on time for three of the seven Board meetings held in the twelve
> months to July 2026.

`PR` Recommendation 3 (the commitment), `03:1` + `04:4` (our counts)

**res-04** · Reports distinguish what the board must decide from what it is merely being told. · shape d · firm
> Your 2023 review found fourteen of nineteen items were for noting and recommended a
> consent agenda. Nine of the fourteen items on your July 2026 agenda were for noting,
> against 300 minutes of scheduled time.

`PR` Recommendation 7 + finding one, `03:2`

**res-05** · The board has the management information it needs to hold the executive to account. · shape a · firm
> Your Q1 commentary describes a solid quarter with improvements in a majority of
> customer-facing measures, while seven of the fifteen measures are worsening, including
> operating margin, interest cover, arrears and void loss.

`03:3` (both the commentary and the table — a contradiction inside one paper)

**res-05 (alternative)** · shape d · firm
> Your 2023 review recommended rebuilding the performance report around target, trend,
> peer benchmark and an executive judgement, capped at twelve pages. Your Q1 2026 report
> carries all four columns and runs to 42 pages.

`PR` Recommendation 2, `03:2` (the columns), `03:1` (42 pages)
*Note: this one shows partial compliance — the format was adopted, the cap was not. Worth
keeping precisely because it is not a simple failure.*

**res-07** · The board has sufficient access to independent advice and assurance. · shape a · firm

`01:4`, `02:4`

**res-08** · Governance support, including the company secretarial function, is effective. · shape e · check

*Flagged: the 2023 review says the Board is "well served by a capable Company Secretary",
